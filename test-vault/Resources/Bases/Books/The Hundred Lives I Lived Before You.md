## Description
**He’s lived a hundred lives. He’d give them all up for one more minute with her.**  
  
Ren has never been able to control his life. Without warning, he wakes somewhere else. A different era. A different world. A different version of everything he Pirates, Planets, Kingdoms he can't name. The people around him never notice. They just keep living, as if the world didn't change overnight.  
  
Then he meets a girl who makes him want to stay in one.  
  
But loving her means risking everything. When Ren discovers the truth about his condition, he's forced to choose between the life he's always wanted and the one person worth losing it all for. He learns that sometimes the only way to find your way home is to let someone else get lost with you.  
  
*The Hundred Lives I Lived Before You* is a genre-bending love story about chasing the right person across the wrong worlds, and wondering if love is strong enough to survive when reality itself refuses to stay still.
